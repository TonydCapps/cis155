1) Hey Tony, I went through your site, and it’s got a lot of personality. You’re clearly thinking through your ideas and building something that reflects your own voice. Here’s my full review.

What I Appreciate:

Your light/dark mode toggle is a nice touch. It shows you’re thinking about usability and accessibility, which is something a lot of people skip.
I like that you’re keeping everything in one place. It’s organized chaos, and I respect that. I’m doing the same thing with my own Area2-155, so I get it.
Your tone is consistent across the site. Whether you’re pitching a service or just talking through ideas, it feels like you. Not like you’re trying to sound like someone else.
Suggestions for Improvement

You need navigation on every page. Once someone clicks away from the homepage, there’s no way back unless they hit the browser button. Even just a “Back to Home” link would help.

Break up your content more. Some pages are just long scrolls with no headers or spacing. Use <h2> tags or even icons to help guide the reader through it.

Keep your styling consistent. The homepage and About Me page feel polished, but the Contact and Ugly pages drop that. Even a shared footer or font would help tie it together.

Bonus Notes (I really liked!)

The Ugly Page made me laugh. It’s giving “I did this on purpose” energy and I’m here for it.

Your Emergency Cyber Response idea is actually solid. You could build that out into a full-service site if you wanted.

You say you don’t know where it’s going, but honestly, that’s what makes it work. It’s exploratory, and it feels real.

Overall, you’ve built something that feels like a working journal. Just tighten up the nav and layout and you’re good. Nice work.
