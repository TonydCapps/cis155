Why Cybersecurity?

Digital threats are everywhere. Cybersecurity protects your privacy, finances, and reputation by reducing risk from common attacks like phishing, credential theft, ransomware, and device compromise.

Real Risks

  • Phishing — deceptive messages that trick people into revealing passwords or clicking malicious links.
  • Weak passwords — reused or simple passwords let attackers pivot across accounts.
  • Unpatched software — outdated apps and devices are easy targets for automated exploits.

Practical Benefits

  • Fewer interruptions and lost time from incidents.
  • Stronger trust with clients, employers, and family.
  • Better control over personal data and finances.

Quick Start Checklist

  1. Use a password manager and enable two factor authentication.
  2. Keep your OS and apps updated; enable automatic updates where possible.
  3. Back up important files to an offline or cloud backup with versioning.
  4. Limit admin privileges on daily accounts; use separate accounts for admin tasks.

Common Questions
How do I know if Im compromised?

Look for unusual account activity, unexpected password resets, or devices acting strangely. If in doubt, change passwords and run scans.

Is antivirus enough?

Antivirus helps but is not a complete solution. Combine it with updates, strong passwords, and backups.
